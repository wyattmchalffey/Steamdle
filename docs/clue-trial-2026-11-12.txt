STEAMDLE — AGENT-CURATED CLUE TRIAL
Prepared November 12, 2026. Local changes only; not committed, pushed or deployed.

DELIVERED
Six games, six authentic Steam-review clues each. 15 complete short reviews and
21 exact contiguous excerpts. Every clue has a unique reviewer within its game,
an individual Steam review link, recommendation ID, source timestamp, character
offsets and a source-text hash. No paraphrased reviews or fabricated quotations.
The wording is stored once in data/clue-trial-2026-11-12.js.

Try docs/clue-trial-2026-11-12.html in a browser. It reveals one clue at a time,
accepts guesses, shows sources/editorial notes after the answer, and saves local
feedback that can be downloaded. The preview makes no changes to live analytics.

UNUSED-GAME EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
All six IDs were absent from every catalog/seed revision in all 42 reachable Git
commits. They are strong unused candidates. The production database was not
directly accessible, so this is not a claim that the full year's published
puzzles were independently audited. The import additionally checks actual DB
history and will not replace a used or already-snapshotted game's clues.

Blue Prince was excluded: it appeared in the May 2025 catalog despite being
missing from the current list. Of the 110 catalog additions, 109 were never
listed in recorded source; Blue Prince is the one known reintroduction.

Legacy last_played_on began May 15, 2025. Some older failed selections were
marked played before clues loaded. Treating those as used is conservative.
Immutable daily_puzzles began November 4, 2026. Existing surviving play dates
were retained by that migration, but older deleted rows may have lost history.
See unused-games-audit-2026-11-12.json for IDs and supporting commit evidence.

SCHEDULING AND IMPORT CHANGES
Unused games precede every repeat, regardless of cached-clue availability.
The six unused trial games receive the order above within the unused pool.
Used priority games receive no special preference. After unused games are
exhausted, repeats follow oldest-use order; adjacent days cannot share an answer.

Catalog placeholders are registered before scheduling, so an unfinished import
or transient Steam failure cannot masquerade as exhaustion and promote repeats.
When no unused game has a usable set, preparation waits for a usable set.
Existing published and prepared snapshots remain unchanged across restarts.

Usage combines immutable snapshot identity, legacy last_played_on across all
rows with the same Steam ID, and optional recovered history. Removed catalog
rows are deactivated rather than deleted, retaining their history. Cached
curated clues load before scheduling and automatic reseeding cannot replace
them. Public review cards label excerpts.

EDITORIAL RESULT
All six have enough material for a useful progression; a small-batch workflow
looks viable. An independent audit caught and corrected one excerpt that
described a reviewer's real job, and replaced a repeated French-aesthetic clue
with a different game system. Mechanical checks alone would not catch those.

This pilot uses popular games with abundant reviews. It does not yet establish
365-game throughput or quality on sparse-review titles. Difficulty remains an
editorial judgment until people try the games. Dark player humor is retained;
Mewgenics's final clue mentions a deity fight but no major story twist.

EVALUATING THE TRIAL
Have players guess before revealing the editorial notes. Record familiarity,
solve clue, the most useful clue, and whether the progression felt fair.
Look for meaningful correct guesses on clues 2–4 and fewer 'None helped'
responses; do not judge only by a higher overall win rate. The six games have
different audiences, so results are directional, not a controlled experiment.
After deployment, existing owner analytics can inspect clue-level outcomes for
each completed day. No live trial outcomes have been collected yet.
