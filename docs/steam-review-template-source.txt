badges, avatar frames, profile/game links and title breadcrumbs. Replace content
with textContent bindings, replace ReviewText ID with a class/data-role, and add
an honest excerpt marker and verified review count. Retain the source classes
and nesting rather than constructing an approximate card in JavaScript.

specific overrides restore the prior 612px stacked card, 32px author avatar,
blue-grey recommendation band and green/red edge. No two-column store layout.
