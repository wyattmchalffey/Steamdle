HISTORY AND INSTALLATION
All 30 IDs were absent from all 42 pre-update catalog revisions. The installer
then checks actual live last_played_on across all rows, including inactive
aliases, plus immutable puzzle snapshots. If any selected game is already used
or reserved, the entire schedule is rejected without altering existing dates.
This guards against unknown live changes beyond the source-history audit.
Legacy records cannot reconstruct deleted pre-migration history by themselves;
the fact these games were never in the source catalog supplies extra evidence.

Installation must occur November 12, before the November 13 UTC reset. It writes
all 30 snapshots in one transaction and preserves today's and past puzzles.
Any old unpublished selection inside the requested range is backed up in
curated_schedule_backups, keyed by version and puzzle date, before replacement.
The installed pack fingerprint prevents later edits under the same version.
Restarts never reroll the month. Cached text means Steam is not needed to serve
these 30 days. The ordinary unused-first policy resumes after the month.
